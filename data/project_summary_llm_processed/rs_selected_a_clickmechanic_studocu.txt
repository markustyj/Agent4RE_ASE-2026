**Title:** ClickMechanic — Summary  

**Overview:**  
ClickMechanic is a platform designed to connect car owners with trusted, professional mechanics for repairs, servicing, and inspections. The platform aims to address the long-standing issue of finding honest and transparent mechanics by offering instant quotes, fast booking, and secure payments. Founded in 2012, ClickMechanic simplifies the process of booking a mechanic, saving customers time and ensuring quality service.  

**Key Points:**  
- **Goals and Scope:**  
  - Provide a simple, transparent, and trustworthy way for car owners to book mechanics.  
  - Serve as a platform for mechanics to find jobs and get paid securely.  
  - Enable administrators to manage the platform and developers to update and maintain it.  

- **Top Features:**  
  - User roles: Customers, Mechanics, Administrators, and Developers.  
  - Core functionalities: Sign-in, booking process, inspections, payments/refunds, repairs, servicing, and appointment management.  
  - Instant price estimation and post-appointment feedback.  

- **Non-Functional Requirements:**  
  - Operates on Windows XP, Vista, Mac OS, and JavaScript-enabled browsers (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, IE8+).  
  - Secure system with no external threats and prevention of multiple accounts for the same user.  
  - Integration with social media platforms like Facebook.  

- **User Documentation:**  
  - Includes "How it works," Support Centre, and Contact Us sections for guidance.  

**Risks and Unknowns:**  
- Assumes the system is secure but lacks detailed security specifications.  
- Dependencies on social media integration and browser compatibility may pose challenges.  
- No explicit mention of scalability or performance targets.  
- Limited details on how feedback from customers will be implemented.
