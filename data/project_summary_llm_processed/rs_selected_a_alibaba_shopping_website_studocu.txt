**Title:** Alibaba Shopping Website — Summary  

**Overview:**  
This Software Requirements Specification (SRS) outlines the development of an e-commerce platform modeled after Alibaba's online marketplace. The platform will support consumer-to-consumer (C2C), business-to-consumer (B2C), and business-to-business (B2B) sales, along with features like electronic payments, product search, and cloud services. The goal is to create a robust, user-friendly system that caters to buyers and suppliers while ensuring secure transactions and efficient operations.  

**Key Points:**  
- **Goals and Scope:**  
  - Focuses on enabling online sales, distribution, and marketing of goods.  
  - Supports Alibaba's core services, including B2B trading, retail sales (AliExpress), and product categorization.  
  - Provides a secure and efficient shopping experience for buyers and suppliers.  

- **Key Features:**  
  - Product configuration: Users can customize products and resolve conflicts in configurations.  
  - Comprehensive product details: Detailed product descriptions and browsing options.  
  - Shopping cart: Add/remove products and manage purchases.  
  - Multiple payment methods: Users can choose from various payment options.  
  - Shipping and tracking: Multiple shipping methods, cost display, and shipment tracking.  
  - Tax calculations: Automatic tax computation and display.  
  - Email notifications: Order confirmations and detailed invoices sent to users.  

- **Non-Functional Requirements:**  
  - Data security and privacy ensured through third-party transaction security.  
  - Operates on basic hardware (512MB RAM, 250GB hard disk) and any OS with a web browser.  

**Risks and Unknowns:**  
- No specific design or implementation constraints beyond security and privacy.  
- Assumptions and dependencies are not specified, leaving potential gaps in understanding external factors.  
- User documentation is not yet defined, which may impact onboarding and usability.
