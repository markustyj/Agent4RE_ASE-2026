**Title:** Amazon Shopping Application — Summary  

**Overview:**  
The Amazon Shopping Application is designed to provide a seamless and user-friendly platform for customers to browse, search, and purchase products, while also empowering sellers with tools to manage their inventory, sales, and customer engagement. The application aims to enhance the online shopping experience through personalized recommendations, secure transactions, and efficient order tracking. By integrating with Amazon's existing systems and services, the application seeks to revolutionize e-commerce for both customers and sellers.  

**Key Points:**  
- **Goals:**  
  - Provide customers with a convenient shopping experience, personalized recommendations, and secure payment processing.  
  - Enable sellers to reach a wider audience, manage inventory, and streamline order fulfillment.  
- **Scope:**  
  - Features include product browsing, search, shopping cart management, secure payment processing, and order tracking.  
  - Integration with Amazon's backend systems, including the Amazon Product Advertising API and Amazon Web Services (AWS).  
- **Top Features:**  
  - Customers: Product search, personalized recommendations, secure checkout, and order tracking.  
  - Sellers: Inventory management, sales tracking, customer engagement tools, and performance analytics.  
- **Non-Functional Requirements:**  
  - Compatibility with iOS and Android devices (latest versions).  
  - Compliance with data protection regulations and industry standards.  
  - Secure integration with third-party payment gateways and shipping providers.  
- **Constraints:**  
  - Active internet connection required for functionality.  
  - Must comply with legal and regulatory requirements for data protection and consumer rights.
